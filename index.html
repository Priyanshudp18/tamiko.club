The full walkthrough

Hygiene, built in

Clean testers. Every visit.

Each product has one tester, so we look after it properly. It is cleaned before it reaches you and again after you try it, and it never goes straight from the tester to your skin.

  • Cleaned between every customer. Lipsticks and balms are wiped and their top layer removed. Bottles, pumps and caps are wiped down.
  • Never straight from the tester. Creams and colours are taken out with a clean spatula onto a palette or the back of your hand. Fragrance goes on a blotter or your wrist.
  • Clean hands, always. Your expert sanitises their hands before touching any product.
  • Full size stays sealed. The product you buy is untouched until it's yours.
