Trying and buying

Do I have to buy anything?

Not with Try with Expert. Try everything and keep nothing if nothing feels right. Your expert packs it all back without a fuss.

What can I try with Tamiko?

Makeup, skincare, fragrance, haircare, bath and body, and grooming. You can get them three ways: Try with Expert (a home trial at a slot you book), Try with Sample (a sample in 60 minutes) or Buy Direct (the full size in 60 minutes). Every product page shows which ways are available.

How many products can I try in one visit?

There's no fixed number. Choose what you're curious about when you book and your expert brings those along with their testers. Not sure where to start? Tell us what you like and we'll suggest a few.

Where do your products come from?

Directly from brands and their authorised distributors and retailers, with a valid invoice for every item. No dupes, no grey-market stock.

Visits and experts

Who comes to my home?

A Tamiko BPC expert: trained across makeup, skincare, fragrance and personal care, background verified, and in Tamiko uniform. You'll get their name before the visit.

Do experts push sales?

No. Experts earn the same whether you buy one product or none, so their advice is honest.

How does scheduling work?

When you book, you'll see the next available slot in your area and pick the one that suits you. We'll confirm it and remind you before your expert arrives.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free of charge, any time before your expert sets out.

Hygiene and safety

How do you keep testers hygienic?

Each product has one tester, and it is cleaned between every customer: lipsticks and balms are wiped and their top layer removed, and bottles, pumps and caps are wiped down. Products are taken out with a clean spatula onto a palette or the back of your hand, never applied straight from the tester, and fragrance goes on a blotter or your wrist. Your expert sanitises their hands first, and the full-size product you buy stays sealed until it's yours.

What if I have sensitive skin or allergies?

Tell your expert before you try anything, and check the ingredient list, which is available for every product. Your expert's guidance isn't medical advice, so if you have a skin condition, check with your dermatologist first.

Payments and returns

When do I pay?

With Try with Expert, you pay at the end of the visit, only for what you keep. For samples and Buy Direct, you pay when you order. UPI and cards are accepted.

Can I return something I bought?

Since you try before you pay, opened products can't be returned. If a sealed product you bought is damaged, expired or not what you ordered, write to us within 48 hours and we'll replace or refund it. Details are in our terms.

Samples and Buy Direct

How fast is delivery?

Samples and Buy Direct orders reach you in 60 minutes in Gurugram. Home trials with an expert happen at the slot you book.

What's a Tamiko sample?

A real sample of a full-size product, so you can try it your way before buying the full size.

Is Buy Direct stock genuine?

Yes. Every full-size product comes sealed, from brands and their authorised sellers, with an invoice.
