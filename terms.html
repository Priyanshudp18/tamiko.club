The service

Tamiko lets you try beauty products at home before you buy them, in two ways:

  • Try with Expert: at a slot you book, a Tamiko BPC expert brings the sealed full‑size products you chose, along with a tester of each, to your address. You try the testers and buy only what you want to keep.
  • Try with Sample: a sample of a full-size product delivered to you, typically within 60 minutes in Gurugram, which you can try before ordering the full size.
  • Buy Direct: sealed full-size products ordered and delivered to you, typically within 60 minutes in Gurugram.

Each product shows which way it can be tried. We're launching in stages, starting in Gurugram, so categories, brands and areas will change over time. Joining the waitlist doesn't guarantee a slot or a launch date in your area.

Product safety

Products are sourced from brands and their authorised sellers. Ingredient lists are available for every product, and you should check them before trying. Tell your expert about any allergy or sensitivity before you try anything. Our experts give product and application guidance, not medical or dermatological advice. If you have a skin condition, consult a qualified professional first. Stop using any product that causes irritation.
