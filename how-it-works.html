How it works

Try it where you'll actually wear it.

Your bathroom mirror. Your window light. Your skin on a normal day. That's where beauty decisions should be made, and that's where Tamiko brings them.

01

Pick what you want to try

Browse makeup, skincare, fragrance, haircare, bath and body, and grooming. Filter by concern, skin type, shade or scent.

Every product carries a tag: Expert Visit for premium picks, Discovery Kit for new brands and everyday finds. Not sure? Ask for suggestions and we'll build your list.

02

Book a slot that suits you

See the next available slot for your area and choose a time. There's no payment to book and no minimum order.

You'll get a confirmation with your expert's name, and a reminder before they arrive.

03

Try everything, properly

Your expert opens a fresh single-use applicator for each tester and helps with shade, texture, finish and how a scent settles. Or they step back and let you explore.

Kits work the same way, just on your schedule. Try the samples over a few days and rate them in the app.

04

Keep what you love

Buy the sealed full-size products you want and pay at the end of the visit. Everything else goes back in the kit.

Keep one, keep all, or keep nothing. The visit is free either way.

An Expert Visit, minute by minute

About 45 minutes. Zero pressure.

An example of how a typical visit runs. Yours moves at your pace.

0′
Your expert arrives

In Tamiko uniform, with your products, testers and a sealed hygiene kit.

5′
A quick chat

Your skin, your routine, what you're hoping to find. Allergies or sensitivities first.

10′
Try, compare, ask

Each tester opened with a fresh applicator. Shades checked in daylight. Scents tried on skin.

35′
Decide

Take your time. Your expert gives an honest take if you want one.

45′
Pay for what you keep

UPI or card. Invoice on the spot. Used applicators bagged and taken away.

Two ways to try

Which one applies?

Expert Visit

For premium and prestige products

  • Sealed full size plus a tester of each product
  • A trained Tamiko BPC expert at your home
  • Free to book, pay only for what you keep
  • Best for foundation, fragrance, serious skincare

Discovery Kit

For new brands and everyday finds

  • Real samples of full-size products
  • Delivered to you, tried on your own time
  • Small kit fee, returned as credit on your order
  • Best for exploring brands you haven't met yet
